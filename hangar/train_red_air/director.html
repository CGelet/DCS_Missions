Set 1: BVR Intercept

CAP

Pilots are told

At CAP, MAGIC starts set 1: Bandit 11, a MiG-29S, 60 km northwest at 25,000 ft, coming straight at you. Build the timeline, shoot outside its missile range, and don't merge.

What actually happens

Reaching CAP activates Bandit 11 and arms the hard-deck watch (flag 30). Bandit 11 dead sets flag 31.

When player flight enters the CAP zone
Activates Bandit 11
RADIOMAGIC: Enfield two, set one. Single group, three-one-zero, thirty-four miles, angels two-five, hot. Fight's on.
Sets flag 30
When Bandit 11 is destroyed
RADIOMAGIC: Enfield two, splash Bandit one-one. Set one complete. Proceed to the BFM point for set two.
Sets flag 31
When your aircraft is below 5,000 ft (sea level) and flag 30 is set and flag 10 is not set
RADIOWREN: Knock it off, knock it off! Enfield two, hard deck. Climb and acknowledge.

BFM

Debrief outcomes

Complete

Complete. All three sets are killed with no hard-deck call. WREN signs off the air-to-air phase, and the aggressor lead buys the first round at Batumi, which almost never happens.

Partial: re-fly items

Partial: re-fly items. Sets 1 and 2 are done, but the 1v2 got away from you, or there was a hard-deck call along the way. You'll re-fly the missed sets with the F-5s on Monday.

Incomplete: re-fly

Incomplete: re-fly the event. Set 1 or 2 isn't finished, or you were shot down. Debrief on the AWACS tapes first: the answer is almost always in the first sixty seconds.
