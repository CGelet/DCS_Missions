Why we're flying this

The last work-up event is the one everyone wants. A partner air force has sent an aggressor detachment to Batumi for the exercise: one MiG-29 and a section of F-5Es flown by pilots who do nothing but fight Western fighters. For the Hornet detachment, it's the closest thing to the real thing short of a war.

RED AIR runs three sets back to back in working area KILO over the sea west of Kobuleti: a beyond-visual-range intercept on the MiG, a one-on-one dogfight with an F-5, then a one-versus-two. MAGIC, the exercise AWACS, controls the sets. WREN grades from the AWACS tapes.

Briefing

Event overview

Single-ship air-to-air training in working area KILO, over the sea 25 to 60 km west of Kobuleti. Climb to CAP at 25,000 ft and check in with MAGIC. Set 1 starts when you reach CAP: Bandit 11, a MiG-29S, starts 60 km northwest at 25,000 ft and flies straight at CAP. When it's dead, go to the BFM point. Set 2 starts when you reach it: Bandit 21, an F-5E, starts 11 km south at 15,000 ft and comes north for a head-on merge. Sixty seconds after Bandit 21 dies, set 3 starts: Bandit 31, two F-5Es, starts 25 km west at 18,000 ft heading east. Hard deck 5,000 ft. MAGIC holds overhead the coast.

Learning objectives

  • primary
    Kill Bandit 11 (BVR) and Bandit 21 (BFM) with no hard-deck knock-it-off.
  • secondary
    Kill both Bandit 31 F-5Es in the 1v2, or terminate with sight on both and energy to separate.
  • tertiary
    Recover to Kobuleti with at least 3,000 lb.

Range and weapons clearance

Weapons free on the aggressor set MAGIC has called "fight's on", inside working area KILO only. Every other aircraft is friendly. Master arm SAFE outside KILO.

Training rules

  1. Hard deck 5,000 ft MSL. Going below it is a training kill on yourself; the sim calls a knock-it-off.
  2. Working area KILO only: west of 41°30'E, north of 41°45'N. Batumi arrivals are below 10,000 ft east of the area.
  3. One set at a time. Don't commit on a set until MAGIC calls it with 'fight's on'.
  4. 1,000 ft bubble: no closer than 1,000 ft to an aggressor. Guns kills are called, not flown to the bubble.
  5. G limit 7.5 g. Over-G is a knock-it-off and an inspection on landing.
  6. Bingo 5,000 lb: knock it off and RTB, whatever set you're on.

Knock-it-off and terminate

Any player calls "Knock it off" for a safety problem: a hard-deck bust, a bubble violation, lost sight close in, an over-G or a warning light. Everyone stops manoeuvring, climbs wings-level and acknowledges by callsign. "Terminate" ends the current engagement only; MAGIC then sets up the next one.

Special subjects

  • MiG-29 versus Hornet in the BVR timeline: where the R-27 outranges you, and how to stay outside it.
  • The F-5 below 300 knots: lighter wing loading, and why you don't slow down with it.
  • 1v2: kill one, but never lose sight of the second. Contracts before shots.
  • Hard deck over the sea: no horizon cues in haze, so use the altimeter and the HUD.

Debrief focus

  • Set 1: the timeline. When you shot, and where the MiG was relative to its own missile range.
  • Set 2: the first turn at the merge. One-circle or two-circle, and why.
  • Set 3: how long you had sight on the second F-5 while you killed the first.
