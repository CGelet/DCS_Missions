Event overview

The training area

Single-ship air-to-air training in working area KILO, over the sea 25 to 60 km west of Kobuleti. Climb to CAP at 25,000 ft and check in with MAGIC. Set 1 starts when you reach CAP: Bandit 11, a MiG-29S, starts 60 km northwest at 25,000 ft and flies straight at CAP. When it's dead, go to the BFM point. Set 2 starts when you reach it: Bandit 21, an F-5E, starts 11 km south at 15,000 ft and comes north for a head-on merge. Sixty seconds after Bandit 21 dies, set 3 starts: Bandit 31, two F-5Es, starts 25 km west at 18,000 ft heading east. Hard deck 5,000 ft. MAGIC holds overhead the coast.

TRAINING RED AIR9 / 23
Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. Hard deck 5,000 ft MSL. Going below it is a training kill on yourself; the sim calls a knock-it-off.
  2. Working area KILO only: west of 41°30'E, north of 41°45'N. Batumi arrivals are below 10,000 ft east of the area.
  3. One set at a time. Don't commit on a set until MAGIC calls it with 'fight's on'.
  4. 1,000 ft bubble: no closer than 1,000 ft to an aggressor. Guns kills are called, not flown to the bubble.
  5. G limit 7.5 g. Over-G is a knock-it-off and an inspection on landing.
  6. Bingo 5,000 lb: knock it off and RTB, whatever set you're on.

Knock-it-off / terminate: Any player calls "Knock it off" for a safety problem: a hard-deck bust, a bubble violation, lost sight close in, an over-G or a warning light. Everyone stops manoeuvring, climbs wings-level and acknowledges by callsign. "Terminate" ends the current engagement only; MAGIC then sets up the next one.

TRAINING RED AIR10 / 23
Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. MiG-29 versus Hornet in the BVR timeline: where the R-27 outranges you, and how to stay outside it.
  2. The F-5 below 300 knots: lighter wing loading, and why you don't slow down with it.
  3. 1v2: kill one, but never lose sight of the second. Contracts before shots.
  4. Hard deck over the sea: no horizon cues in haze, so use the altimeter and the HUD.
