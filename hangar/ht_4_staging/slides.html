Previously

The SA-10 behind Ochamchire lost its track radar to Springfield's HARMs while it was busy with the decoys. The battery is blind, and the harbour is within reach.

OP HIGH TIDE2 / 19
The story so far

With the SA-10 blind, Ochamchire is reachable. PRISM's latest picture shows three landing ships alongside the quay now, loading vehicles day and night. Fishing boats report the harbour's guns and SAMs all point out to sea, because that's where everything has come from so far.

OP HIGH TIDE3 / 19
Why it matters

So Springfield won't come from the sea. MERLIN's plan crosses the coast at Anaklia in Georgian territory and runs north behind the coast, low, to come at the harbour from the hills. It's forty kilometres of flying low over somebody else's farmland.
