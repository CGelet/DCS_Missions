Debrief outcomes

Success

Two landing ships burn alongside the quay, and the third is holed and listing. The landing force has lost half its lift in five minutes. Whatever's left will have to sail soon or not at all.

One landing ship is hit, but the others pull off the quay and anchor offshore. The landing will go ahead, smaller.
