The story so far

With the Gumista holding, the coalition moved the Stennis strike group into the eastern Black Sea to put fighters over Sukhumi around the clock. The answer from the north was always going to come by air. Overnight, two Tu-22M3 Backfires moved forward. Each carries a Kh-22 that can hit a carrier from 300 km away.

Situation

The battlespace

The Stennis strike group is operating 70 nm west of Sukhumi, steaming southwest into the wind. Long-range aviation has staged Tu-22M3 bombers within reach of the eastern Black Sea. Intelligence expects a raid this evening, most likely from the northwest over the water, but the mountains to the north can't be ruled out. Red fighters have escorted bombers before. Shell 1 provides big-wing gas south of the boat. Texaco 1, overhead Mother, carries a small amount for emergencies.
