CAP Station

CAP NCAP S

Debrief outcomes

Success

Both Backfires go into the sea north of the CAP before they reach launch range. The strike group never goes to general quarters. You trap on the last of the light, and BISHOP is waiting on the flight deck.

Partial

One Backfire gets its missile off before you reach it. The escorts knock the Kh-22 down twelve miles from the boat. Nobody aboard sleeps tonight, and the admiral wants a second CAP station by morning.

Failure

The raid reaches launch range untouched. Two Kh-22s come in at Mach 3, and one gets through the escorts' defences. The Stennis is still afloat but out of the fight, and so is the coalition's air cover over Sukhumi.
