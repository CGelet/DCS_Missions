Previously

Both Transcam bridges went into the Great Liakhvi before the main body could cross. The brigade stopped at Java and sent for its engineers.

OP IRON GATE2 / 19
The story so far

The bridges slowed the column, but they didn't stop it. Engineers had a crossing in by yesterday afternoon, and something came over it with them. Last night a Georgian Su-25 on a recce run up the valley was shot down at 18,000 ft, far above anything a Strela can reach. The pilot's last call was "big radar, big missile."

OP IRON GATE3 / 19
Why it matters

That's an SA-11. It changes everything: nobody can work the valley until it's gone. ELINT has heard it radiate from three different places in the last day. It moves after it shoots.

Situation

The battlespace

An SA-11 battery is covering the Great Liakhvi valley between Java and Tskhinvali. It shot down a Georgian Su-25 last night at 18,000 ft. ELINT has three candidate sites, all on high ground beside the valley; it relocates after shooting. RIVET will give you a bearing on the live emitter when you reach HUNT. Expect point defence near the battery, and the column's Strelas in the valley floor.
