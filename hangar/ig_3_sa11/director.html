The Hunt

HUNT

Pilots are told

At HUNT, RIVET will give you a bearing on the live radar. HARM it from outside 35 km if you can, then follow up with JSOWs on the launchers. Don't descend into the valley to look.

What actually happens

The rolled site spawns at HUNT with RIVET's bearing (see variants). Battery 75% dead: success (flag 10).

When the target is destroyed
RADIORIVET: Enfield, Rivet. Buk radar off the air and it's not coming back. Good kill.

Debrief outcomes

Success

The SA-11's radar and launchers burn on the hillside, and the valley's high sky belongs to the coalition again. Gori asks for CAS the same evening.

Partial

Enfield's HARMs silence the radar, but the launchers survive and the battery is back on the air by morning with a spare. The valley stays dangerous for another day.

Failure

The SA-11 is still there and still moving. Every sortie into the valley now has to plan around a battery nobody can find.
