The story so far

At 0200 the Roki tunnel border post stopped answering the phone. By 0400, Georgian police at Tskhinvali's northern checkpoint were reporting engine noise in the gorge: tracks, a lot of them, coming south. Nobody has seen the column yet. The valley is under cloud, and the drones that went up at first light came back with nothing but grey.

The detachment at Vaziani was due to fly home in two weeks. Instead, the Georgian government has asked the coalition for help, and Enfield flight is going up the valley to find out how much is coming, and how far it got in the night.

Briefing

Situation

An armoured column of unknown size is moving south through the Roki tunnel and down the Great Liakhvi valley toward Tskhinvali and Gori. Its lead elements could be anywhere between the tunnel and Tskhinvali's northern edge. Georgian forces hold Gori and the villages north of it. Expect short-range air defence with the column. Nothing has been seen in the air.

Objectives

  • primary
    Find the column's vanguard and destroy it.
  • secondary
    Get ATFLIR video of the column behind the vanguard for Gori.
  • tertiary
    Recover both aircraft to Vaziani.

Rules of engagement

Weapons free on military vehicles moving south on the Transcam road north of Tskhinvali. Weapons tight in Tskhinvali itself and south of it: Georgian police and civilians are on the roads. No release north of the Roki tunnel.
