OP DEEP CUT1 / 21
The story so far

The two MiG-29s at Gudauta have shown up on every mission this month. They don't fly much because they don't have much fuel. Every night a few tanker trucks come down the coast road from Gagra to keep them topped up.

OP DEEP CUT2 / 21
Why it matters

Burn the fuel and the MiGs stay in their shelters. But since the Bzyb strike, the coast has been wall-to-wall air defence. So this time the Harriers go in the back door: over the mountains, down the Bzyb valley, and onto Gudauta from the north, where nobody is looking.

Situation

The battlespace

Red keeps two MiG-29s at Gudauta, fuelled by tanker trucks that arrive from Gagra overnight. The coast between Pitsunda and New Athos is covered by layered air defences, but the mountains to the north are not. SPARROW, a special-forces team on the ridge north of the airfield, will report where the fuel is when you're inbound. Arco 1, a KC-130, is the only tanker available and sits east of the mountains.
