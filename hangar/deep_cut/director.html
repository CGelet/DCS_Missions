Debrief outcomes

Success

The fuel goes up in a column of black smoke you can see from Sukhumi. For the next ten days, the Gudauta MiGs stay in their shelters, and SPARROW walks out over the ridge without being seen.

Partial

Some of the fuel burns, some gets away. The MiGs fly again in three days, fewer and shorter. HATCH starts planning the next back-door run.

Failure

The trucks are untouched and the MiGs keep flying. Worse, red now knows the back door exists, and SPARROW has to come out the hard way.
