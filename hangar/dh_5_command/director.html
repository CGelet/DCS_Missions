The Command Post

CPROAD

Pilots are told

SA-8 first with the Maverick, then the GBU-31 on the courtyard and GBU-12s on the radio trucks. If the launcher is on the road, it's the priority: find it on ROAD before it reaches open ground.

What actually happens

Command post 70% destroyed: flag 11. The launcher is handled (flag 12) if it was parked at the CP, or once it dies on the road; reaching LAUNCH POINT first means it fires (flag 20). Flags 11 and 12 with no launch: success (flag 10).

RADIOCAPT. LOMIDZE: Pontiac, Lomidze. Their command net just went silent. All of it.

Debrief outcomes

Success

The command post burns and the last launcher burns with it, or on the road where it stopped. The missile brigade stops being a brigade. DARK HORSE is over, and Kutaisi's runway reopens the next day.

Partial

The command post is hit but the last launcher fires before it's found. The missile falls short of Kutaisi in a field. It's the last one, but nobody knew that at the time.

Failure

The command post survives and the last launcher fires at Kutaisi. It hits the detachment's old hangar. Nobody is hurt, because nobody's been there for a week.
