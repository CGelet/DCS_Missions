How to fly these
  1. A single mission: download its .miz into Saved Games/DCS/Missions/ and open it from the Mission menu.
  2. A campaign: copy its folder from hangar/campaigns/ into Saved Games/DCS/Missions/Campaigns/ and pick it from the Campaign menu.
  3. Read the briefing or present the slide deck before you fly. Pilots: don't open the director pages, they're spoilers.
