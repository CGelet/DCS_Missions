The story so far

Three weeks ago a ceasefire along the Inguri line collapsed. Separatist forces, re-equipped with modern air defences and backed by Russian advisers, have been pushing armour south down the coastal M-27 highway toward Sukhumi. The coalition has held them at the Gumista, but intelligence shows a fresh mechanised brigade staging near Gagra for a push within 36 hours.

The coast road crosses the Bzyb River on a single heavy-load bridge, the only crossing that can carry tanks and bridging equipment. Drop it, and the brigade is stuck on the wrong side of the river for a week.

Briefing

Situation

Red mechanised forces are staging north of the Bzyb River for an offensive toward Sukhumi. The M-27 road bridge at Bzyb is their only heavy crossing. Blue holds air superiority over the coast. Point-defence AAA at the bridge is confirmed from imagery; a mobile SA-6 battery was seen in the area 36 hours ago and hasn't been located since. Red has kept fighters at Gudauta, but nobody has seen them fly this week.

Objectives

  • primary
    Destroy the Bzyb River road bridge.
  • secondary
    If the SA-6 is still in the area and engages, destroy or suppress it.
  • tertiary
    Return both aircraft to Senaki-Kolkhi.

Rules of engagement

Weapons free on positively identified red air and air defence systems. Weapons tight on all ground targets other than the bridge and its defences. No releases south of the Gumista River. Avoid civilian traffic on the M-27.
