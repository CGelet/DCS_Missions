The story so far

Three weeks ago a ceasefire along the Inguri line collapsed. Separatist forces, re-equipped with modern air defences and backed by Russian advisers, have been pushing armour south down the coastal M-27 highway toward Sukhumi. The coalition has held them at the Gumista, but intelligence shows a fresh mechanised brigade staging near Gagra for a push within 36 hours.

OP BROKEN SPAN2 / 23
Why it matters

The coast road crosses the Bzyb River on a single heavy-load bridge, the only crossing that can carry tanks and bridging equipment. Drop it, and the brigade is stuck on the wrong side of the river for a week.

Situation

The battlespace

Red mechanised forces are staging north of the Bzyb River for an offensive toward Sukhumi. The M-27 road bridge at Bzyb is their only heavy crossing. Blue holds air superiority over the coast, but the target is covered by a mobile SA-6 battery and point-defence AAA. Red keeps a MiG-29 alert pair at Gudauta.

Debrief

Mission success

The bridge is down. Recon from a coalition drone at 0815 shows the brigade's lead elements backed up along the M-27 north of Bzyb, with engineers already surveying the riverbank. Brenner's men on the Gumista get the week they needed.

OP BROKEN SPAN21 / 23
Debrief

Partial success

The bridge is damaged but still standing. Light vehicles are getting across. HATCH is already writing a re-attack tasking for this afternoon, and the SA-6 crews now know you're coming.

OP BROKEN SPAN22 / 23
Debrief

Mission failed

The bridge stands. By nightfall the first T-72s are across the Bzyb, and the Gumista line is bracing for contact at dawn.
