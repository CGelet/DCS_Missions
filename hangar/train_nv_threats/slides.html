Event overview

The training area

Two-ship low-level through Kawich Valley, 160 km northwest of Nellis. Transit high to WEST, then drop to the valley floor at DROP and fly north up the valley at 500 ft AGL and 480 kt. An SA-6 emitter in Gold Flat sits behind the western ridge; an SA-8 emitter sits behind the eastern ridge. Both radiate and hold fire. Above 7,000 ft MSL in the valley before POP, Blackjack calls a valid shot. At POP, pull up and attack the target group at the north end with Mk-82s: three trucks and a Shilka hulk. Egress low to the east through the gap, then climb home.

Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. Threat emitters are live and radiating. They never fire. Blackjack calls a valid shot if they see you.
  2. Masking: below 6,500 ft MSL (about 1,000 ft AGL) in the valley, the ridges hide you. Above 7,000 ft MSL before POP is a valid shot.
  3. Minimum altitude 500 ft AGL in the valley. The sim calls an abort below 200 ft AGL.
  4. After POP, the emitters are masked out of the exercise: climb for the attack as briefed.
  5. No-fly: Groom Lake restricted area (R-4808N), 27 km east of DROP.
  6. Bingo 4,500 lb to Nellis. Wingman stays in a loose trail on the low-level, 1 nm behind.

Knock-it-off / terminate: Blackjack or either pilot can call "Knock it off" for anything unsafe: a minimum-altitude bust, losing sight of the leader in the valley, or a bird strike. Climb to 15,000 ft, master arm SAFE, and wait for Blackjack. A valid shot is not a knock-it-off: you keep flying and it goes on the grade sheet.

Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. RWR picture: the SA-6's search radar (Straight Flush) and the SA-8 (Land Roll). Which spike means you're seen?
  2. Ridge crossing: roll inverted and pull over a ridge, don't float over it.
  3. Pop-up geometry: from POP, pull to 30° nose up, roll in at 4,000 ft AGL, 20° dive on the target.
  4. Wingman in the valley: trail, not formation. If you lose sight, climb only on the leader's call.
