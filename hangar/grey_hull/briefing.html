The story so far

After the Backfire raid, the Black Sea Fleet stopped sending bombers and started sending ships. Last night a surface action group left Novorossiysk under cover of the weather: a Krivak-class frigate and a Grisha corvette, heading southeast to put SA-N-4s and a towed sonar between the Stennis and the coast.

They know about the Hawkeye, so they're hiding in the shipping lanes. At 0500 the E-2C had two surface groups 25 miles apart off Gelendzhik, each about the size of a frigate and a corvette. One is the warships and the other is merchants bound for Novorossiysk. Springfield flight has four Harpoons and has to work out which is which.

Briefing

Situation

A red surface action group (one Krivak frigate and one Grisha corvette) sortied from Novorossiysk overnight and is somewhere in the shipping lanes off Gelendzhik. DARKSTAR holds two surface contacts of similar size about 25 nm apart: ALPHA to the north and BRAVO to the southeast. One is the warships, the other is merchant traffic. Both have SA-N-4 class air defence if they're warships. The weather is low and wet, so expect to find them by radar and ESM, not by eye. Krymsk has Su-27s that have flown over the fleet before. The Stennis is 100 nm southeast, steaming northwest into the wind.

Objectives

  • primary
    Disable the surface action group (at least half the group's damage).
  • secondary
    Do not hit merchant shipping.
  • tertiary
    Recover both aircraft aboard the Stennis.

Rules of engagement

Weapons free on the warships once you've identified them by ESM, radar or sight. Weapons hold on any contact you can't identify. A Harpoon will hit the first ship its seeker finds: don't launch it through a merchant. Weapons free on red aircraft that DARKSTAR declares hostile.
