Under the Radar

LOWHOOK

Pilots are told

Drop to 500 ft below the cloud and run in under the ships' radar horizon. From HOOK both contacts are inside Harpoon range and outside SA-N-4 range. Use your radar, RWR and ATFLIR to confirm which one is the warships before you commit.

What actually happens

The RWR should pick up the warships' radars from HOOK. A decoy emitter isn't modelled, so the RWR tells the truth even when DARKSTAR doesn't. The AI wingman only engages armed ships.

When player flight enters the HOOK zone
RADIODARKSTAR: Springfield, Darkstar. You're at the hook. Your call, your shot.
