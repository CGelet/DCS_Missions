TRAINING CHECKRIDE10 / 23
Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. Package of three flights on one timeline. You lead the strike (Colt 1). Uzi 1 escorts, Weasel 1 suppresses.
  2. Time on target is 3:20 after PUSH, ±30 s. A strike outside the window is graded as a miss, even if the bombs hit.
  3. The range SA-8 at the target radiates on weapons hold. Weasel 1 is cleared to kill it; you are not.
  4. The two F-5E aggressors are live-fire: DCS has no simulated-kill mode. They're Uzi's problem first, yours if they leak.
  5. Minimum 15,000 ft MSL over the target for the JDAM release. No releases outside TARGET AREA.
  6. Bingo 4,500 lb to Kutaisi.

Knock-it-off / terminate: Anyone in the package calls "Knock it off" for a midair risk, a hung store or a leaker on your tail with your bombs still on. Strike flight turns west at 20,000 ft, master arm SAFE, and waits for WREN on det common.

TRAINING CHECKRIDE11 / 23
Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. Push timing: you control the TOT with speed after PUSH, not with turns over the target.
  2. If the escort is busy: who calls the leaker, and does the strike abort or press?
  3. GBU-38 on trucks: one bomb per vehicle, release inside the LAR, don't wait for the pod.
  4. Egress: the F-5s will be behind you. Speed and altitude home.
