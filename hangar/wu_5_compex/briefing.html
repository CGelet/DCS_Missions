Why we're flying this

This is the last event of work-ups and the one WREN signs the whole card on. It puts everything from the last three weeks into one sortie: a package that has to arrive together, an emitter that has to be suppressed, aggressors that want to break it up, and a target that has to be hit on time.

The target set is a line of old vehicles in open fields southwest of Khashuri, standing in for a fuel and ammunition point. The emitter is the range's SA-8, and the aggressors are the contract F-5 pilots the det will fight in February. You lead the strike.

Briefing

Event overview

Strike two-ship from Kutaisi with GBU-38s against a simulated fuel and ammunition point in open fields southwest of Khashuri. Uzi 1 (escort) and Weasel 1 (SEAD) launch with you and push together from PUSH, 43 km west of the target. TOT is 3:20 after PUSH. A weapons-hold SA-8 sits at the target; two live-fire F-5E aggressors come from the north when the package pushes.

Learning objectives

  • primary
    Destroy the target set with JDAMs inside the TOT window.
  • secondary
    Lose no strike aircraft to the aggressors.
  • tertiary
    Recover both jets to Kutaisi above bingo.

Range and weapons clearance

GBU-38s on the target vehicles inside TARGET AREA only. Air-to-air against the aggressors after MAGIC calls them hostile. The SA-8 is Weasel's: strike flight doesn't engage it.

Training rules

  1. Package of three flights on one timeline. You lead the strike (Colt 1). Uzi 1 escorts, Weasel 1 suppresses.
  2. Time on target is 3:20 after PUSH, ±30 s. A strike outside the window is graded as a miss, even if the bombs hit.
  3. The range SA-8 at the target radiates on weapons hold. Weasel 1 is cleared to kill it; you are not.
  4. The two F-5E aggressors are live-fire: DCS has no simulated-kill mode. They're Uzi's problem first, yours if they leak.
  5. Minimum 15,000 ft MSL over the target for the JDAM release. No releases outside TARGET AREA.
  6. Bingo 4,500 lb to Kutaisi.

Knock-it-off and terminate

Anyone in the package calls "Knock it off" for a midair risk, a hung store or a leaker on your tail with your bombs still on. Strike flight turns west at 20,000 ft, master arm SAFE, and waits for WREN on det common.

Special subjects

  • Push timing: you control the TOT with speed after PUSH, not with turns over the target.
  • If the escort is busy: who calls the leaker, and does the strike abort or press?
  • GBU-38 on trucks: one bomb per vehicle, release inside the LAR, don't wait for the pod.
  • Egress: the F-5s will be behind you. Speed and altitude home.
