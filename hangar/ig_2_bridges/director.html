Pop and Release

POPBRIDGES

Pilots are told

Pop to 15,000 ft at POP and run northwest. Release inside the JDAM's range: lead on BRIDGE NORTH, wingman on BRIDGE SOUTH. Then turn hard south and out, above the Strelas.

What actually happens

Each bridge dead sets flag 41 / 42; both completes the mission (flag 10). The main body reaching NORTH APPROACH while BRIDGE NORTH stands is the failure ending (flag 20).

Debrief outcomes

Success

Both spans are in the river. The brigade's main body stops at Java, and its engineers start looking for a crossing. Gori has bought two days.

Partial

One bridge is down, but the column finds a way around it on the old road. It loses a day, not two.

Failure

The main body crosses the north bridge before it falls. By nightfall the brigade is between Java and Tskhinvali, and the bridges no longer matter.
