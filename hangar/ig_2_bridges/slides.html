The story so far

Yesterday's tape changed the conversation in Tbilisi. It isn't a raid: it's a mechanized brigade, and its main body is still in the gorge north of Java, waiting for fuel and for the road to clear. Between it and Tskhinvali, the Transcam road crosses the Great Liakhvi twice on two concrete bridges.

OP IRON GATE3 / 19
Why it matters

Drop both, and the brigade has to wait for engineers. Drop them after it's crossed, and there's nothing between it and Gori but the Georgian 4th Brigade. Nobody knows when it will start moving.

Situation

The battlespace

The column's main body is in the gorge north of Java. South of Java, the Transcam road crosses the Great Liakhvi on two concrete road bridges: BRIDGE NORTH, 4 km south of Java, and BRIDGE SOUTH at Gupta, 6 km further on. Drop both before the main body reaches them. It hasn't moved since this morning, but it could start at any time. Expect the column's Strelas along the road and the vanguard's survivors near Gupta.
