The story so far

At 2204 a missile came down on the edge of Kutaisi airfield. It missed the runway by 400 metres and took out a fuel farm. Georgian radar at Senaki tracked it all the way: a ballistic missile, a Scud, launched from the hills north of Ochamchire. By the time anyone looked, the launcher had gone.

OP DARK HORSE2 / 18
Why it matters

Scuds don't stay where they fire. They shoot, drive to a hide, and wait for the next order. The hide sites are on the mountain roads, under the trees, and they move at night. The detachment moved its Hornets to Senaki two hours ago. Pontiac flight is going to look.

Situation

The battlespace

A Scud launcher fired at Kutaisi at 2204 from the hills north of Ochamchire, then disappeared. It is probably in a hide on one of the mountain roads between Ochamchire and the upper Kodori, within about 15 km of the launch point. No moon tonight. Expect point defence at the hide: guns, maybe a short-range SAM. Gudauta's fighters have not flown at night so far.
