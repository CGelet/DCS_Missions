The story so far

The depot burned, and the launchers went quiet. Then, two nights ago, they started moving again, and always just before Pontiac's jets arrived. Capt. Lomidze found out why: a long-range early warning radar has been set up on a peak above Tkvarcheli, 2,000 metres up. It sees everything that lifts off from Senaki and passes the warning down the valley.

OP DARK HORSE3 / 19
Why it matters

It's on one of two peaks; ELINT can't separate them. It can see anything high from 200 km away. It can't see into the Inguri valley. So that's how Pontiac is going in: up the valley in the dark, under its horizon, and over the ridge at the last minute.

Situation

The battlespace

An early-warning radar is on one of two peaks above Tkvarcheli, at 2,000 to 2,500 m. It can't be separated by ELINT. It sees anything high from Senaki, but not into the Inguri valley. Fly north up the Inguri past Jvari at 2,000 ft AGL in the dark, climb over the ridge west of Khaishi, and attack the radar from the east with JSOWs or laser bombs. Expect guns and possibly a short-range SAM at the site.

Night Launch
