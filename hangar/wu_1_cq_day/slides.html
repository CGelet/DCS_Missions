The training area

Single ship from Kobuleti to the Stennis, operating 25 km west of Batumi and steaming into the wind (from 300 at 12 kt) at about 20 kt. Case I recovery: two touch-and-goes, then two arrested landings, with a catapult launch between them. TEXACO, an S-3B tanker, orbits overhead the boat at 6,000 ft. TRAINING PADDLES9 / 22
Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. Case I: arrive at the initial 3 nm astern at 800 ft, 350 kt. Break inside 4 nm, abeam at 600 ft.
  2. Two touch-and-goes first (hook up), then hook down for two arrested landings. The sim counts traps only.
  3. Between traps: taxi to a catapult and launch. A trap only counts after you've climbed above 1,000 ft again.
  4. Wave-offs are mandatory. Bolter: power up, climb straight ahead to 600 ft, turn downwind.
  5. Bingo 4,000 lb to Kobuleti (TACAN 67X, 60 km east). TEXACO is overhead the boat at 6,000 ft if you need gas.

Knock-it-off / terminate: PADDLES or the pilot can call "Knock it off": a fouled deck, a hydraulic caution or a pilot who's behind the jet. Climb to 2,000 ft, orbit overhead the boat to port, and wait. Divert to Kobuleti if fuel goes below bingo before the deck reopens.

TRAINING PADDLES10 / 22
Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. Lineup: the deck is moving away from you to the right. Fly the ball and the line, not the boat.
  2. The burble: 12 kt of wind plus 20 kt of ship gives a big burble in close. Small power corrections.
  3. Fuel for the pattern: each pass costs about 400 lb. Count your passes before you need TEXACO.
  4. Cat shot: hands off the stick, salute when you're ready, and fly the climb at 15° nose-up.
