Why we're flying this

The detachment landed at Kutaisi last week with ten Hornets and a February partner exercise to get ready for. The USS John C. Stennis is passing through the eastern Black Sea for a port call, and the strike group has agreed to keep a deck open for the detachment for one afternoon. The Hornets are land-based for this deployment, but every pilot has to stay current at the boat, and the February exercise will have carrier play.

It's day, the sea is calm, and the deck is yours for an hour. PADDLES, the strike group's landing signal officer, will grade every pass. WREN will be on the platform next to him.

Briefing

Event overview

Single ship from Kobuleti to the Stennis, operating 25 km west of Batumi and steaming into the wind (from 300 at 12 kt) at about 20 kt. Case I recovery: two touch-and-goes, then two arrested landings, with a catapult launch between them. TEXACO, an S-3B tanker, orbits overhead the boat at 6,000 ft. Bingo is 4,000 lb to Kobuleti, 60 km east.

Learning objectives

  • primary
    Two arrested landings aboard the Stennis, with a catapult launch between them.
  • secondary
    Two touch-and-goes before the first trap, graded by PADDLES.
  • tertiary
    Never below bingo without a plan: TEXACO or Kobuleti.

Range and weapons clearance

No weapons. Master arm SAFE. Follow Mother's and PADDLES' instructions: a wave-off is mandatory.

Training rules

  1. Case I: arrive at the initial 3 nm astern at 800 ft, 350 kt. Break inside 4 nm, abeam at 600 ft.
  2. Two touch-and-goes first (hook up), then hook down for two arrested landings. The sim counts traps only.
  3. Between traps: taxi to a catapult and launch. A trap only counts after you've climbed above 1,000 ft again.
  4. Wave-offs are mandatory. Bolter: power up, climb straight ahead to 600 ft, turn downwind.
  5. Bingo 4,000 lb to Kobuleti (TACAN 67X, 60 km east). TEXACO is overhead the boat at 6,000 ft if you need gas.

Knock-it-off and terminate

PADDLES or the pilot can call "Knock it off": a fouled deck, a hydraulic caution or a pilot who's behind the jet. Climb to 2,000 ft, orbit overhead the boat to port, and wait. Divert to Kobuleti if fuel goes below bingo before the deck reopens.

Special subjects

  • Lineup: the deck is moving away from you to the right. Fly the ball and the line, not the boat.
  • The burble: 12 kt of wind plus 20 kt of ship gives a big burble in close. Small power corrections.
  • Fuel for the pattern: each pass costs about 400 lb. Count your passes before you need TEXACO.
  • Cat shot: hands off the stick, salute when you're ready, and fly the climb at 15° nose-up.
