December 2037. Before the detachment goes to Georgia, it spends three weeks at Nellis, flying on ranges the size of small countries. WREN has built the schedule around the things the Black Sea won't give them time to practise, and the first is moving targets.

Where it fits

Everything the detachment has dropped on so far has stood still. Real targets don't. On Range 61B, in Tikaboo Valley, the range crew has three groups of target vehicles driven by remote control up and down the dry valley floor. Two Mavericks of each type, a full gun, and one rule: hit the group Blackjack calls, and nothing else.

TRAINING RANGE 61B4 / 23
Event overview

The training area

Single-ship moving-target qualification on Range 61B, Tikaboo Valley, 110 km north of Nellis. Check in with Blackjack at 61B IP at the south end of the valley. Three groups of remote-driven target vehicles drive north-south on the valley floor: M-1 (three trucks), M-2 (two BMPs) and M-3 (two jeeps, faster than the others). Blackjack clears them hot in that order: Maverick F on M-1, Maverick E on M-2, then the gun on M-3. HOLD, east of the valley, is the high key. Nothing on the range shoots.

TRAINING RANGE 61B9 / 23
Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. Cleared hot only on the group Blackjack calls. Hitting any other group is a range foul, and the range goes cold.
  2. Run in along the valley, north or south. No east-west run-ins: the targets drive north-south and you'll lose them.
  3. Minimum altitude 500 ft AGL on the range. The sim calls an abort below 150 ft AGL.
  4. No releases north of HOLD's latitude. The Extraterrestrial Highway, 15 km north, is a public road.
  5. No-fly: Groom Lake restricted area (R-4808N), 30 km west. No entry at any altitude.
  6. Laser code 1688 for the Maverick E. Check it matches in the pod and on the missile.
  7. Bingo 4,000 lb to Nellis.

Knock-it-off / terminate: Blackjack or the pilot can call "Knock it off" for anything unsafe: a hung missile, an altitude bust, losing sight of the range boundaries or a misidentified group. Climb to HOLD altitude (15,000 ft), master arm SAFE, and wait for Blackjack. "Range is cold" means master arm SAFE until cleared hot again.

TRAINING RANGE 61B10 / 23
Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. Maverick F (IR) lock on a moving truck: the seeker tracks heat, so lock the engine, not the cargo bed.
  2. Maverick E (laser): the pod must track the mover. Use the ATFLIR's area track, then point track, before you fire.
  3. Strafing a mover: lead it. At 450 kt and 30 mph the jeep moves 15 m during the bullets' flight.
  4. Dust: the convoys raise dust on the lake bed. It hides them from the pod and shows them to your eyes.

Event 3: Strafe
