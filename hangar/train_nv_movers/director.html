Debrief outcomes

Complete

Complete. All three groups destroyed in order, with no fouls and no aborts. WREN signs off moving-target qualification, and the detachment's Maverick shots go into the Georgia plan.

Partial: re-fly items

Partial: re-fly items. At least one group got away, or the pass had a foul or an abort. The missed events go on Thursday's schedule.

Incomplete: re-fly the event. A range foul ended the period or R-4808N was entered. The re-fly comes after a talk with the Nellis range officer.
