Why we're flying this

A month before the deployment turns hot, the Hornet detachment is in Georgia for the partner exercise, flying from Kutaisi. The detachment's training officer wants every pilot current on the two skills that get people killed when they're rusty: plugging a tanker basket, and flying low and fast through terrain that doesn't forgive mistakes.

LONG VALLEY puts both in one sortie. You'll take gas from the exercise tanker over Imereti, then run a timed low-level route up the Dzirula valley, over the Surami ridge and down the Borjomi gorge to a simulated attack outside Akhaltsikhe. Nothing on this sortie is hidden. What's graded is how precisely you fly it.

Briefing

Event overview

Two-ship training sortie from Kutaisi. After departure, join ARCO 1 on the refuelling track north of the field at 20,000 ft and take 6,000 lb each. Then descend to enter the low-level route at Zestaponi: 500 ft AGL, 420 knots, four timed checkpoints, ending with a simulated attack (TOT, no weapons released) on a marked truck west of Akhaltsikhe. Climb out to the northwest and return to Kutaisi. The route is clear of other traffic. All times on the route run from crossing ZESTAPONI.

Learning objectives

  • primary
    Fly the low-level route with at least 3 of 4 checkpoints and the TOT on time (±30 s), with no altitude knock-it-off.
  • secondary
    Refuel from ARCO 1: rendezvous, stable pre-contact, 6,000 lb on-load.
  • tertiary
    Recover to Kutaisi as a section with at least the bingo fuel.

Range and weapons clearance

No live ordnance is carried. The TOT is a simulated attack: run in, mark the target with the ATFLIR, and call "Bombs away" on the det frequency. Air-to-air missiles are carried for weight and balance only. Master arm stays SAFE.

Training rules

  1. Minimum altitude 500 ft AGL on the low-level route; 1,000 ft AGL over towns. The sim calls a knock-it-off below 200 ft AGL.
  2. Maximum 450 KIAS below 1,000 ft AGL.
  3. Pass checkpoints abeam within 1 nm, never overhead below 1,000 ft AGL. Each checkpoint has a 2 km timing gate.
  4. Tanker: no closer than pre-contact without ARCO's clearance. One receiver on the boom area at a time.
  5. Bingo 4,500 lb for an immediate climb and direct return to Kutaisi.
  6. Weather minimums for the low level: 3,000 ft ceiling and 8 km visibility. Below that, fly the route at 5,000 ft AGL.

Knock-it-off and terminate

Any player calls "Knock it off" for anything unsafe: an altitude bust, lost sight in the gorge, a bird strike, or a warning light. Everyone climbs wings-level to 1,000 ft AGL minimum and acknowledges by callsign. Call "Terminate" to end one leg and keep the event going.

Special subjects

  • Spatial disorientation in a narrow valley: trust the radar altimeter and the HUD, not the horizon.
  • Turning in the Borjomi gorge: start the turn early, and if in doubt, climb and turn.
  • Tanker overrun: what you do when you're closing too fast at pre-contact.
  • Lost route: climb to 5,000 ft AGL, call it, and rejoin at the next checkpoint.
