The story so far

The launcher in the hills is gone, and Capt. Lomidze has an answer to her question: there are more. A captured driver says the brigade has four launchers and one place they all go back to for fuel and missiles, a depot in the orchards west of Gali. Kill the depot and the launchers run dry.

OP DARK HORSE3 / 19
Why it matters

The catch is the weather. A front came in off the sea this afternoon and parked over Abkhazia: cloud at 4,500 ft, drizzle under it, and dusk. Laser bombs from above are no good. Pontiac flight is going under the deck.

Situation

The battlespace

The Scud brigade's fuel and missile depot is in farmland 6 km west of Gali, beside a country road: fuel bowsers, missile transporters and a crane truck under nets. Overcast at 4,500 ft with drizzle and dusk light: attacks have to be flown under the cloud. Expect anti-aircraft guns at the depot and possibly something better. Gudauta's MiG-29s have flown at dusk before.
