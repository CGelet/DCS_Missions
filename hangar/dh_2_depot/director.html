Debrief outcomes

Success

The depot burns all night, and Gali can see the glow from the orchards. With no fuel and no reloads, the brigade's launchers go quiet for three days.

Partial

Half the depot burns. The brigade gets the rest out in trucks before dawn. The launchers keep firing, less often.

Failure

The depot is untouched. The next morning a Scud lands in Senaki's dispersal area.
