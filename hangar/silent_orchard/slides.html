The story so far

Two days after the strike on the Bzyb bridge, the separatists answered. Yesterday at 1410 a coalition MQ-9, MAGPIE 3, went silent over the hills above New Athos. Its last telemetry frame shows a 9S18 search radar locking it up from somewhere in the orchards below.

Why it matters

An SA-11 changes everything on this coast. Until it's found and killed, nothing slow or low flies near the Gumista: no drones, no tankers, no close air support for Brenner's company on the river. The battery has moved twice since the shoot-down. ELINT has a rough area and a lot of guesses.

Situation

The battlespace

A mobile SA-11 (Buk) battery shot down an MQ-9 near New Athos yesterday and has relocated at least twice since. Its current position is unknown; ELINT puts it somewhere in a 10 km area between New Athos and the Achandara road. The Gumista front has been quiet in the air since. Red still has fighters at Gudauta, and there are unconfirmed reports of short-range air defences moving up behind the river.
