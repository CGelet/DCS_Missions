Previously

Two of the three landing ships at the Ochamchire quay burned. What's left of the landing force has to sail soon, or not at all.

OP HIGH TIDE2 / 19
The story so far

At 0300, Ochamchire emptied. What was left of the landing force, the ship that survived the strike and one that came up from Sochi, sailed south in the dark with a corvette for escort. There's only one beach worth landing on: the long sand at Anaklia, forty kilometres away. At ten knots, they'll be on it at breakfast.

OP HIGH TIDE3 / 19
Why it matters

USS Ross has been shadowing them since they sailed. She's a destroyer, not an attack ship, and the strike group won't let her start a gunfight with a corvette. That job is Springfield's. The problem is that Ross is out there in the dark, somewhere near the target.
