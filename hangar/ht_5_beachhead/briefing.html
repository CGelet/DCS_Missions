The story so far

At 0300, Ochamchire emptied. What was left of the landing force, the ship that survived the strike and one that came up from Sochi, sailed south in the dark with a corvette for escort. There's only one beach worth landing on: the long sand at Anaklia, forty kilometres away. At ten knots, they'll be on it at breakfast.

USS Ross has been shadowing them since they sailed. She's a destroyer, not an attack ship, and the strike group won't let her start a gunfight with a corvette. That job is Springfield's. The problem is that Ross is out there in the dark, somewhere near the target.

Briefing

Situation

Two landing ships and a corvette are steaming south-southeast from Ochamchire toward the beach at Anaklia, about 40 km away, at 10 kt. USS Ross is shadowing them; MERLIN will tell you where she is when you check in. The corvette has SA-N-4 and guns. Gudauta's Su-30s are expected to cover the landing. Sink the landing ships before they reach the beach.

Objectives

  • primary
    Sink both landing ships before they reach the beach at Anaklia.
  • secondary
    No weapons on USS Ross: Harpoons only if MERLIN clears them.
  • tertiary
    Recover both aircraft aboard the Stennis.

Rules of engagement

Weapons free on the landing force. Harpoons only when MERLIN confirms USS Ross is clear of the convoy; otherwise visual identification on the pod and laser or Maverick weapons only.
