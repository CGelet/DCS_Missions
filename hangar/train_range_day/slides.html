Why we're flying this

Two days after LONG VALLEY, the detachment moves its weapons work east. The Georgian Land Forces have lent the exercise a tactical range on the steppe near Udabno: open, flat and empty, with hulks and old trucks laid out as targets. It's the best range the detachment has flown on in a year.

TRAINING GAREJA RANGE2 / 23
Where it fits

Today is a qualification. Three events in order, one pilot at a time: a laser-guided bomb on armour, unguided bombs on trucks, and the gun. Range control runs the pattern. Nothing out there shoots back. What's graded is accuracy and range discipline.

Event overview

The training area

Single-ship qualification on GAREJA RANGE, 25 km east of Vaziani. Check in at GAREJA IP at 15,000 ft. Range control clears the events in order: Event 1, GBU-12 on T-1 (two tank hulks). Event 2, Mk-82 CCIP on T-2 (three trucks). Event 3, strafe on T-3 (two light vehicles). The run-in is from the northwest on heading 120. HOLD, to the east, is the high key for the pattern. The targets are inert hulks and don't shoot back. You're the only aircraft on the range for this period.

TRAINING GAREJA RANGE9 / 23
Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. Cleared hot only on the target range control calls. Hitting any other target is a range foul, and the range goes cold.
  2. Run-in heading 120 ±20° for every event. Left-hand pattern, with HOLD as the high key.
  3. Minimum release altitude 3,000 ft AGL for Mk-82s. Strafe foul line 2,000 ft slant range. The sim calls an abort below 150 ft AGL.
  4. No-fly area: 5 km around David Gareja monastery, 14 km south of the range. No overflight at any altitude.
  5. Laser code 1688. Lase only on the run-in heading.
  6. Bingo 3,500 lb to Vaziani. Vaziani is 25 km west: you'll get there.

Knock-it-off / terminate: Range control or the pilot can call "Knock it off" for anything unsafe: a hung store, an altitude bust, a misidentified target or another aircraft in the pattern. Climb to HOLD altitude (15,000 ft), master arm SAFE, and wait for range control. "Range is cold" means master arm SAFE until you're cleared hot again.

TRAINING GAREJA RANGE10 / 23
Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. Hung bomb: jettison over the range impact area only, then go straight back to Vaziani.
  2. Target fixation on the strafe pass: recover by the foul line, whatever the bullets are doing.
  3. Self-lasing a GBU-12: start lasing at 10 seconds to impact and keep the spot on the tank.
  4. The wind: 300 at 8 kt is a crosswind on a 120 run-in. Where will the Mk-82s go?
