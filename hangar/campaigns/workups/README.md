# WORK-UPS: Hornet detachment syllabus, January 2038

Training campaign, 5 chapters, 2038-01-15 to 2038-01-30.

> Ten Hornets, three weeks and one instructor. Before the February partner exercise, every pilot in the detachment flies the same five events: day and night carrier quals, SEAD, CAS with a JTAC, and a graduation checkride.

## Install

1. Copy this folder (`workups.cmp` and the numbered `.miz` files) into `Saved Games/DCS/Missions/Campaigns/`.
2. In DCS, open **Campaign** and pick **WORK-UPS**.
3. Pass a chapter to unlock the next. Fail it and DCS gives you the same chapter again.

For the full campaign page, download the repo and double-click `Mission Hangar.html` in its top folder. Pilots: don't open the director pages before you fly.
