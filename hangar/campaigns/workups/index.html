WORK-UPS

Hornet detachment syllabus, January 20262026-01-15 → 2026-01-30Kutaisi, Vaziani, Senaki and the Stennis
Ten Hornets, three weeks and one instructor. Before the February partner exercise, every pilot in the detachment flies the same five events: day and night carrier quals, SEAD, CAS with a JTAC, and a graduation checkride.

Why this syllabus

The detachment arrived at Kutaisi in the second week of January with ten F/A-18Cs, a container of spares and a partner exercise in February that will put them alongside Georgian, Turkish and Romanian crews. LCDR Ana "WREN" Castellano, the detachment's training officer, has three weeks to make sure every pilot is current and safe in everything the exercise will ask of them.

Her syllabus builds from the boat outward: landing on a ship by day and by night, shooting radars, working with a controller on the ground, and finally a checkride that puts it all on one timeline. Each event is graded. Pass one to move on to the next.
