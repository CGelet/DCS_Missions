# DARK HORSE: Ten nights hunting Scuds, June 2038

Campaign, 5 chapters, 2038-06-02 to 2038-06-12.

> Mobile Scud launchers in the hills of Abkhazia are firing at Kutaisi. Pontiac flight hunts them at night, under low cloud and through rain: the launchers, the depot that feeds them, the radar that warns them, and finally the headquarters that sends them.

## Install

1. Copy this folder (`dark_horse.cmp` and the numbered `.miz` files) into `Saved Games/DCS/Missions/Campaigns/`.
2. In DCS, open **Campaign** and pick **DARK HORSE**.
3. Pass a chapter to unlock the next. Fail it and DCS gives you the same chapter again.

For the full campaign page, download the repo and double-click `Mission Hangar.html` in its top folder. Pilots: don't open the director pages before you fly.
