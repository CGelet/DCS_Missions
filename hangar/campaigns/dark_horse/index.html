DARK HORSE

Ten nights hunting Scuds, June 20382038-06-02 → 2038-06-12Senaki-Kolkhi, Kobuleti and Batumi
Mobile Scud launchers in the hills of Abkhazia are firing at Kutaisi. Pontiac flight hunts them at night, under low cloud and through rain: the launchers, the depot that feeds them, the radar that warns them, and finally the headquarters that sends them.

The story

After HIGH TIDE failed, the war changed shape again. On the night of 2 June a ballistic missile came down on the edge of Kutaisi airfield, and Georgian radar traced it back to the hills north of Ochamchire. The coalition detachment pulled its Hornets back to Senaki and Kobuleti and started looking for something that only moves in the dark.

Over ten nights, Pontiac flight learns how the missile brigade works and takes it apart one piece at a time. Most of it is flown at night or in weather nobody would choose. None of the targets want to be found.
