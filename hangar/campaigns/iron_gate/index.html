IRON GATE

Ten days in the Liakhvi valley, April 20382038-04-06 → 2038-04-16Vaziani and Kutaisi, central Caucasus
An armoured brigade comes south through the Roki tunnel toward Gori. The Hornet detachment at Vaziani was two weeks from going home. Find the column, slow it, clear the sky above it, hold the town, and close the tunnel behind it.

The story

In the early hours of 6 April, the Roki tunnel border post stopped answering the phone. By dawn, Georgian police north of Tskhinvali could hear tracks in the gorge. The coalition detachment at Vaziani, ten Hornets that had spent the winter on exercises, was the only air power close enough to do anything about it.

Over ten days, Enfield flight flies every kind of mission the valley asks for. The brigade adapts after each one: it brings engineers when the bridges fall and a long-range SAM when the jets come, and it attacks Gori when it has nothing left to wait for. The detachment adapts back.
