# IRON GATE: Ten days in the Liakhvi valley, April 2038

Campaign, 5 chapters, 2038-04-06 to 2038-04-16.

> An armoured brigade comes south through the Roki tunnel toward Gori. The Hornet detachment at Vaziani was two weeks from going home. Find the column, slow it, clear the sky above it, hold the town, and close the tunnel behind it.

## Install

1. Copy this folder (`iron_gate.cmp` and the numbered `.miz` files) into `Saved Games/DCS/Missions/Campaigns/`.
2. In DCS, open **Campaign** and pick **IRON GATE**.
3. Pass a chapter to unlock the next. Fail it and DCS gives you the same chapter again.

For the full campaign page, download the repo and double-click `Mission Hangar.html` in its top folder. Pilots: don't open the director pages before you fly.
