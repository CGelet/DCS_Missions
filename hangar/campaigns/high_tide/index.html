HIGH TIDE

Ten days off the Abkhaz coast, May 20382038-05-11 → 2038-05-20USS John C. Stennis, eastern Black Sea
Landing ships are gathering at Ochamchire, forty minutes from the flat Georgian beaches between Anaklia and Poti. From the Stennis, Springfield flight counts them, clears the approaches, blinds the battery that guards them, hits them alongside the quay, and stops what sails.

The story

A month after the Roki tunnel, the war moved to the coast. The northern brigade had failed to reach Gori by land, and somebody decided to try the sea. Through the first week of May, fishermen out of Anaklia watched grey ships with bow doors come into Ochamchire's small harbour and not leave.

The Stennis came back into the Black Sea on 8 May. Over ten days its air wing does what a carrier is for: it finds out what's there, takes away the defences one at a time, and makes sure that when the landing force finally sails, it doesn't arrive.
