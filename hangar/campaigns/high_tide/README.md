# HIGH TIDE: Ten days off the Abkhaz coast, May 2038

Campaign, 5 chapters, 2038-05-11 to 2038-05-20.

> Landing ships are gathering at Ochamchire, forty minutes from the flat Georgian beaches between Anaklia and Poti. From the Stennis, Springfield flight counts them, clears the approaches, blinds the battery that guards them, hits them alongside the quay, and stops what sails.

## Install

1. Copy this folder (`high_tide.cmp` and the numbered `.miz` files) into `Saved Games/DCS/Missions/Campaigns/`.
2. In DCS, open **Campaign** and pick **HIGH TIDE**.
3. Pass a chapter to unlock the next. Fail it and DCS gives you the same chapter again.

For the full campaign page, download the repo and double-click `Mission Hangar.html` in its top folder. Pilots: don't open the director pages before you fly.
