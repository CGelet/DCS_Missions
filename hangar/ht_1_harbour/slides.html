The story so far

A month after the Roki tunnel, the war moved to the coast. Fishing boats out of Anaklia have been reporting grey ships at Ochamchire all week: big ones, with bow doors. Ochamchire's harbour is too small for a navy, but it isn't too small for landing ships, and the Georgian coast from Anaklia to Poti is flat, sandy and forty minutes away.

OP HIGH TIDE2 / 18
Why it matters

The Stennis came back into the Black Sea three days ago. Before anyone plans anything, the strike group needs to know what is in that harbour. Nobody has a picture newer than a week. Today, Springfield flight gets one.

Situation

The battlespace

Ochamchire, in red-held Abkhazia, 120 km north of the carrier. Fishing boats report landing ships in the harbour this week; nobody has seen them from the air. The harbour is small and shallow, with a quay along its north side; the Kodori river mouth and its anchorage are 25 km up the coast to the northwest. Expect point air defence at the harbour and a patrol boat. Gudauta has MiG-29s on alert, 90 km northwest.
