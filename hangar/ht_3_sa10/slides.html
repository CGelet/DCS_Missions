OP HIGH TIDE1 / 19
Previously

Three minelayers went down off Poti before they could finish a line. Georgia's biggest port is still open.

OP HIGH TIDE2 / 19
The story so far

Poti's channels are open. The night after the minelayers, a Georgian Su-25 looking at the coast north of Anaklia saw a missile come up from the hills behind Ochamchire, from 60 km away. It missed, barely. ELINT confirmed it within the hour: an SA-10, the long-range S-300, moved in to cover the harbour.

OP HIGH TIDE3 / 19
Why it matters

Nothing gets near Ochamchire until it's blind. The S-300 is patient: it won't radiate for a lone jet it can see coming. So today, Springfield brings it something more interesting to look at: a sky full of decoys.

OP HIGH TIDE5 / 19
Situation

The battlespace

An SA-10 (S-300PS) battery is on high ground behind Ochamchire, covering the harbour and the coast out to 75 km. ELINT has it at one of two hill sites. It radiates only briefly. Fly low over the water to LAUNCH, pop to 20,000 ft, launch TALDs toward the harbour, and kill the track radar with HARMs when it lights up for them. Expect point defence at the battery.

Launch

Cold start on the Stennis. Launch and go straight down to 500 ft over the water heading north. Below the S-300's horizon until you pop.

Phase 2 · 10:40

Pop and Decoy

Pop to 20,000 ft at LAUNCH and release all six TALDs toward the harbour. RIVET will call the search radar the moment it lights up. That tells you which site.
