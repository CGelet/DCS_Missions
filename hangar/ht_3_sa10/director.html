Debrief outcomes

Success

The SA-10's track radar takes two HARMs and stops radiating for good. Without it, the battery is a field of very expensive launchers. The air over Ochamchire is open.

Partial

The HARMs damage the battery but the track radar survives. It's off the air for a day while they repair it, and the strike group has to move fast.

Failure

The battery never takes the bait, or it shoots down the decoys and the HARMs miss. Ochamchire stays out of reach, and the landing force keeps loading.
