The script

10:00

Start and Transit

Pilots are told

Cold start at Vaziani. Load both emitters as pre-planned HARM targets before you taxi. Depart east and climb to 24,000 ft for SPARTAN IP.

What actually happens

WREN checks in from the range cabin.

When 10 s after mission start
RADIOWREN: Dodge one, Wren at Spartan. Radars are warming up. They'll be looking at you before the IP.
10:22

Shot 1: SA-6 Radar

SPARTAN IPSHOT 1

Pilots are told

Check in at SPARTAN IP. Range control clears the range hot. SHOT 1 is 35 s from the IP. Fire the first HARM at the SA-6 radar, and turn away west while it flies.

What actually happens

SPARTAN clears the range hot at the IP (flag 50). The SA-6 radar dying sets flag 11.

When player flight enters the SPARTAN IP zone
RADIOSPARTAN: Dodge one, Spartan. Range is hot. Cleared SA-6 radar and SA-8. Launchers are not cleared.
Sets flag 50
When SPARTAN Radar is destroyed and flag 50 is set
RADIOSPARTAN: Direct hit on the SA-6 radar. It's off the air. Dodge one, continue to Shot 2.
Sets flag 11
10:26

Shot 2: SA-8

SHOT 2

Pilots are told

SHOT 2 is 3 minutes from the IP: turn away after Shot 1, then come back round to it. Fire the second HARM at the SA-8 and turn away. Don't close inside 10 km of it to make the shot.

What actually happens

The SA-8 dying after the SA-6 radar completes the event (flag 10). Any launcher hit is a foul (flag 22).

When SPARTAN Osa is destroyed and flag 11 is set
RADIOSPARTAN: SA-8 destroyed. Both emitters down. Range is cold. Dodge one, cleared off to the west.
Sets flag 10
When SPARTAN Launchers is below 100% life
RADIOSPARTAN: Dodge one, you hit a launcher. That's a range foul. Range is cold, master arm safe.
Sets flag 22
When your aircraft is below 5,000 ft above the ground and player flight enters the RANGE zone and flag 50 is set
RADIOSPARTAN: Dodge one, below the range floor. Climb, climb.
Sets flag 23

Off the range to the west via EGRESS, master arm SAFE, and recover to Vaziani.

Debrief outcomes

Complete

Complete. Both radars are smoking holes, both shots were on time, and the launchers are untouched. WREN signs SEAD-1 and the range officer asks for a copy of your tape.

Partial: re-fly items

Partial: re-fly items. One emitter is still radiating, or a shot was outside its window. You'll re-fly the missed shot on the next range day.

Incomplete: re-fly

Incomplete: re-fly the event. A launcher hit or a border infringement closed the range. The re-fly comes after a sit-down with WREN and SPARTAN.
