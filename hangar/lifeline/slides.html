The story so far

At 0735, Uzi 2-2, an F-16 on a road recce north of the Gumista, took a missile nobody saw launch. Capt. Mara "SOCKS" Lindqvist ejected over the hills above Eshera, four miles behind the line. Her beacon is good and she's checked in twice on guard. The second time she said she could hear engines.

OP LIFELINE2 / 20
Why it matters

The rescue helicopter at Sukhumi is twelve minutes away, but it won't go into a valley nobody has looked at, and the search parties will be on the roads by now. The alert Hornets at Kutaisi are the closest guns. Pontiac flight is rolling.

Situation

The battlespace

Uzi 2-2's pilot is on the ground in the hills above Eshera, about 6 km behind the Gumista line, in red-held territory. Her position is known from her beacon. Red has light forces in Novy Afon and the upper Gumista valley, and both have roads that lead toward her. Nobody saw what shot her down. Pedro 1 is on alert at Sukhumi-Babushara, 30 km south, and will launch when the area around her is clear. Gudauta is 30 km west and has fighters.

On Scene

Set up a wheel over SANDY at 15,000 ft, above anything that shot Uzi 2-2. Call SOCKS on guard: she can tell you where the noise is coming from. Expect vehicles on the roads, from the west or from the north.

Phase 4 · 09:26

Sanitize

Find the vehicles and stop them before they reach her. Hit the lead vehicle to block the road. Positively identify everything near the survivor. Pedro launches only when you call the area clean.

Phase 5 · 09:45

Pedro In

Pedro comes in low along the coast and up the Eshera valley. Stay overhead and watch his route: whatever shot Uzi 2-2 down may still be on the ridge between Pedro and the survivor. He'll be on the ground for about 90 seconds.

Home
