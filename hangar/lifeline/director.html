Debrief outcomes

Success

Pedro 1 lifts SOCKS off the hillside and is feet-dry at Sukhumi eighteen minutes later with a sprained ankle and a story. The search party's wrecks are still on the road above Eshera at sunset. Uzi squadron buys Pontiac's bar tab for a month.

Partial

Pedro makes the pickup but takes hits coming out and puts down hard short of Sukhumi. Everyone walks away, but the coalition is one rescue helicopter short, and RAWHIDE wants every future pickup escorted all the way in.

Failure

The search party reaches the hillside first. That evening, Russian state TV shows a coalition pilot in a Gudauta hangar. The ceasefire talks now have a new item on the agenda, and it isn't the Gumista.
