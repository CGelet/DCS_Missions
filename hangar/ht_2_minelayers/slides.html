Previously

Springfield's photo pass over Ochamchire brought home the count: landing ships and patrol boats in a harbour too small for a navy, and vehicles waiting on the quay.

OP HIGH TIDE2 / 19
The story so far

Springfield's tape showed landing ships at Ochamchire. Two nights later, Georgian fishermen coming home to Poti saw something worse: fast boats with no lights, running slowly in lines across the harbour approaches and dropping things off their sterns.

OP HIGH TIDE3 / 19
Why it matters

If Poti's approaches are mined, nothing gets in or out of Georgia's biggest port, and the coalition can't land so much as a crate there. The boats have to be stopped before they finish tonight. They're small, fast and dark, and the Stennis has two Hornets on deck with Mavericks.

Situation

The battlespace

Night, a bright moon, calm sea. Small fast boats are laying mines in one of Poti's two approach channels: the northern one, off the Rioni mouth, or the southern one, off Kulevi. DARKSTAR has intermittent small surface contacts near Poti but can't hold them. A Georgian coast guard boat is in the harbour mouth: it's friendly. Find the minelayers with the radar and the pod, and sink them before they finish a line.
