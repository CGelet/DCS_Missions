Night Launch

MARSH

Pilots are told

Cold start on deck, night launch. Join at MARSH and push east at 15,000 ft. Radar in sea mode, pod in IR. The boats will be the only things moving slowly with no lights.

What actually happens

The channel is rolled at start; the boats are already on their way. 40%: a decoy boat runs south toward Batumi.

When 10 s after mission start
RADIOMERLIN: Springfield, Merlin. Poti says the boats are still out there. Go.
When 5 s after mission start · 40% chance

The Hunt

HUNT

Pilots are told

Search the approaches from HUNT at 12,000 ft. Slow contacts in a line near the channel are what you're after; anything running fast may be a decoy. Identify on the pod, then shoot.

What actually happens

Poti Coast Guard calls when you reach HUNT. They hear engines but don't know which channel.

When player flight enters the HUNT zone
RADIOPOTI COAST GUARD: Springfield, Poti Coast Guard. We hear engines out there, low and slow. We can't see anything. We're lit up in the harbour mouth: don't shoot us.

Stop the Laying

Pilots are told

Sink the lead boat first: it's the one that knows where the line goes. Watch your altitude: their guns reach 4,000 ft.

What actually happens

Minelayers destroyed: success (flag 10). A minelayer group reaching its MINEFIELD zone first is the partial ending (flag 20). Any hit on the coast guard: flag 22.

When the target is destroyed
RADIOPRISM: Springfield, Prism. Darkstar's lost the slow contacts. They're gone. Good shooting.

Debrief outcomes

Success

Three boats burn off Poti, and the channel they were seeding has only the first few mines in it. Georgian divers mark them by noon. The port stays open.

Partial

The boats are stopped, but they finish their first line. Poti's northern channel is closed for a week while sweepers work it. Ships still get in by the other channel.

Failure

The boats finish both lines and run home. Poti is closed. The coalition's supplies for Georgia now come through Batumi, three times further from where they're needed.
