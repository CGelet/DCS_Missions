Why we're flying this

The Georgian 2nd Infantry Brigade is on a field exercise south of the Gareja range, and they've offered their joint terminal attack controllers to the detachment for an afternoon. The rules are simple: they'll pretend to be in contact, put old vehicles out as the enemy, and talk you onto them the way they would in a real fight. Their own platoon will be sitting right next to the targets.

The JTAC is Captain Giorgi Kapanadze, callsign AXEMAN. He's done this in Afghanistan, with real friendlies. He won't clear you hot until he's sure you're looking at the right thing.

Briefing

Event overview

Single ship from Vaziani to CONTACT POINT, 35 km east, over the steppe south of the Gareja range. A Georgian platoon (three Bradleys and a Humvee) holds the track junction. AXEMAN will pass two 9-lines in turn: trucks north of the friendlies first, then vehicles dug in east of them, closer. Every target is inert and won't shoot back. Attack headings north-south only.

Learning objectives

  • primary
    Destroy both target groups on AXEMAN's clearance.
  • secondary
    Correct 9-line readbacks and north-south attack headings.
  • tertiary
    No damage to the friendly platoon.

Range and weapons clearance

Release only on AXEMAN's "cleared hot". Friendly platoon at the track junction: positive identification of the target on every pass. Master arm SAFE outside the attack run.

Training rules

  1. No weapons release without AXEMAN's "cleared hot". "Continue" means fly the pass with master arm SAFE.
  2. Friendlies: the platoon in the three Bradleys and a Humvee at the track junction. Every attack heading must be parallel to the friendly line (north-south).
  3. Read back the 9-line's lines 4, 6 and 8 (elevation, target location, friendlies) before you're cleared.
  4. Minimum 2,000 ft AGL for rocket and gun passes, 5,000 ft AGL for bombs.
  5. Hitting the friendly platoon is a foul and ends the event.
  6. Bingo 3,500 lb to Vaziani, 25 km west.

Knock-it-off and terminate

AXEMAN or the pilot calls "Abort, abort, abort" for anything pointing at the friendlies, and "Knock it off" for anything unsafe. Climb to 15,000 ft over CONTACT POINT, master arm SAFE, and wait for AXEMAN.

Special subjects

  • Talk-ons: from big to small. Find the track junction, then the dry stream bed, then the vehicles.
  • Danger close: 500 m from friendlies for a GBU-12. What changes in your attack geometry?
  • Rockets versus the gun versus a GBU-12 on two trucks in a ditch.
  • Wind: 310 at 10 kt pushes unguided weapons toward the friendlies on the second target.
