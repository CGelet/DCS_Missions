The story so far

Gori held, and the northern brigade has run out of ways forward. It still has tanks in Tskhinvali and an attack battalion licking its wounds at Ergneti, but everything it needs to keep fighting comes through one tunnel and stops at one dump: a field of fuel trucks and ammunition south of Java.

Col. Gelashvili's request is short. Burn the dump, and hit whatever is queued at the tunnel's south portal waiting to come through. Then the brigade has a week of fuel and nowhere to get more. The tunnel is close to the border, and the airfields on the other side have fighters.

Briefing

Situation

The northern brigade's main supply dump is on open ground south of Java: fuel bowsers and ammunition trucks under nets, defended by an SA-15. Vehicles queue at the Roki tunnel's south portal, 25 km north, waiting to come through. Both MiG-29s at Beslan and Su-27s at Nalchik have flown over the tunnel this week; both are inside 100 km. You'll come over the Racha range from the west at 30,000 ft and recover to Vaziani.

Objectives

  • primary
    Destroy the supply dump south of Java.
  • secondary
    Destroy the vehicles queued at the tunnel's south portal.
  • tertiary
    Recover both aircraft to Vaziani.

Rules of engagement

Weapons free on the dump, the portal queue and any military vehicle on the Transcam road north of Tskhinvali. No release north of the tunnel. No pursuit of aircraft north of the main ridge.
