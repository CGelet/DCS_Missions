Why it matters

Col. Gelashvili's request is short. Burn the dump, and hit whatever is queued at the tunnel's south portal waiting to come through. Then the brigade has a week of fuel and nowhere to get more. The tunnel is close to the border, and the airfields on the other side have fighters.

Situation

The battlespace

The northern brigade's main supply dump is on open ground south of Java: fuel bowsers and ammunition trucks under nets, defended by an SA-15. Vehicles queue at the Roki tunnel's south portal, 25 km north, waiting to come through. Both MiG-29s at Beslan and Su-27s at Nalchik have flown over the tunnel this week; both are inside 100 km. You'll come over the Racha range from the west at 30,000 ft and recover to Vaziani.
