The script

10:50

Launch

AMBROLAURI

Pilots are told

Cold start at Kutaisi. Northeast to Ambrolauri climbing to 28,000 ft. Load the dump as the JDAM target and the portal as the second JSOW target.

What actually happens

SABRE launches you. The fighter variant is rolled at mission start and plays at IP RACHA.

When 10 s after mission start
RADIOSABRE: Enfield, Sabre. Last one. Close the gate and come home to Vaziani.

Over Racha

ONIIP RACHA

Pilots are told

East over Oni at 30,000 ft and 480 kt. At IP RACHA, WIZARD will tell you if anything's coming off Beslan or Nalchik. You'll have the bombs on when it does.

What actually happens

WIZARD's call at IP RACHA depends on the rolled fighters (see variants).

RADIOWIZARD: Enfield, Wizard. Picture clean for now. Beslan and Nalchik both have jets on alert.

Debrief outcomes

Success

The dump at Java burns for two days. Without fuel, the northern brigade starts pulling its tanks back toward the tunnel on the third. IRON GATE is over; the gate is closed.

Partial

Part of the dump survives, and the brigade rations what's left. It holds Tskhinvali for another week before the talks in Geneva do what the bombs didn't.

Failure

The dump is untouched and the trucks keep coming through the tunnel. The war in the valley settles into a stalemate that lasts the summer.
