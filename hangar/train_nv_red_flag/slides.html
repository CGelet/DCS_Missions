Why we're flying this

The last week at Nellis is Red Flag: a large-force exercise with fighters, Wild Weasels, an AWACS and a full squadron of aggressors whose only job is to make everyone else's day difficult. For the detachment it's the dress rehearsal for Georgia: a package, a push time and a target that has to be hit inside a 30-second window, while somebody else fights the war overhead.

TRAINING RED FLAG9 / 22
Training rules

How we fight safe

  1. The aggressors are live in the sim and will shoot. Treat every red contact as hostile once WIZARD declares it.
  2. TOT is 200 seconds after you cross PUSH (13:43:20 on a 13:40:00 push), ±15 s. The sim times you over TGT, not the bomb impacts.
  3. Strikers don't commit on bandits unless they're inside 10 nm and hot. Uzi owns the air.
  4. The range SA-6 radiates but holds fire. It's Weasel's target; strikers don't attack it.
  5. No-fly: Groom Lake restricted area (R-4808N). The route via MARSHAL keeps you clear; don't cut the corner to PUSH.
  6. Bingo 4,500 lb to Nellis. Below joker, strikers jettison and go home.

Knock-it-off / terminate: Anyone in the package can call "Knock it off" for a midair risk, a lost wingman in the fight or a no-fly infringement. Strikers climb to 24,000 ft heading south, master arm SAFE, and wait for WIZARD. The aggressors don't know the knock-it-off call: if they're still coming, defend.

TRAINING RED FLAG10 / 22
Special subjects

Talk it through

  1. Losing a fighter: if Uzi is shot down, the strikers press on unless WIZARD calls the bandits within 10 nm.
  2. Timing: you're 30 seconds late at IP. How much faster to make the window?
  3. Deconfliction: Weasel is at 22,000 ft, you're at 20,000 ft, Uzi is above both.
  4. The JDAM launch zone at 20,000 ft and 450 kt, and what a bandit merge does to it.
