Why we're flying this

The last week at Nellis is Red Flag: a large-force exercise with fighters, Wild Weasels, an AWACS and a full squadron of aggressors whose only job is to make everyone else's day difficult. For the detachment it's the dress rehearsal for Georgia: a package, a push time and a target that has to be hit inside a 30-second window, while somebody else fights the war overhead.

Dodge flight are the strikers. Uzi's F-15s will clear the air ahead of them; Weasel's F-16s will keep the range's SA-6 busy. All Dodge has to do is be on time, put four JDAMs each on the target complex in Gold Flat, and get home. The aggressors will be waiting north of the target.

Briefing

Event overview

Large-force exercise in the north ranges. Dodge flight, the two-ship strike element, launches from Nellis, routes via MARSHAL to stay clear of R-4808N, and crosses PUSH at 13:40:00. TOT on the target complex in southern Gold Flat is 13:43:20 ±15 s: six vehicles in one line. Uzi (two F-15Cs) sweeps ahead; Weasel (two F-16Cs) suppresses the range SA-6 near the target. WIZARD runs the picture. Two aggressor MiG-29s launch from the north when the package pushes and are live in the sim.

Learning objectives

  • primary
    Destroy the target complex with the bombs arriving inside the TOT window.
  • secondary
    Both strikers survive the aggressors.
  • tertiary
    Cross PUSH on time, stay clear of R-4808N, and recover to Nellis.

Range and weapons clearance

Weapons free on the target complex and on aggressors declared hostile by WIZARD. No weapons on the range SA-6 (Weasel's target). Master arm SAFE until PUSH.

Training rules

  1. The aggressors are live in the sim and will shoot. Treat every red contact as hostile once WIZARD declares it.
  2. TOT is 200 seconds after you cross PUSH (13:43:20 on a 13:40:00 push), ±15 s. The sim times you over TGT, not the bomb impacts.
  3. Strikers don't commit on bandits unless they're inside 10 nm and hot. Uzi owns the air.
  4. The range SA-6 radiates but holds fire. It's Weasel's target; strikers don't attack it.
  5. No-fly: Groom Lake restricted area (R-4808N). The route via MARSHAL keeps you clear; don't cut the corner to PUSH.
  6. Bingo 4,500 lb to Nellis. Below joker, strikers jettison and go home.

Knock-it-off and terminate

Anyone in the package can call "Knock it off" for a midair risk, a lost wingman in the fight or a no-fly infringement. Strikers climb to 24,000 ft heading south, master arm SAFE, and wait for WIZARD. The aggressors don't know the knock-it-off call: if they're still coming, defend.

Special subjects

  • Losing a fighter: if Uzi is shot down, the strikers press on unless WIZARD calls the bandits within 10 nm.
  • Timing: you're 30 seconds late at IP. How much faster to make the window?
  • Deconfliction: Weasel is at 22,000 ft, you're at 20,000 ft, Uzi is above both.
  • The JDAM launch zone at 20,000 ft and 450 kt, and what a bandit merge does to it.
